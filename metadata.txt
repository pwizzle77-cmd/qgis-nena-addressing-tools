about=Day-to-day 911 addressing in QGIS, following the NENA NG9-1-1 layout for road centerlines and site/structure address points.
    - Keep one master list of street names. Roads and addresses link to it by StreetID, renames update every linked feature, and duplicates are blocked by a county-wide or per-community rule.
    - Range road centerlines (hundred-block, distance, proportional, or from address points). Ranges are divided automatically when you split, merge, or add a road at an intersection.
    - Place an address by clicking the road and then the building: the house number, side, ZIP, ESN, and community are filled in. Place apartment and townhome units under one number.
    - Draw a new road for a street name and it is named and ranged for you.
    - Drop-down lists for street types, communities, ZIP codes, ESNs, and other fields, so values are entered the same way every time.
    - Check for range gaps, overlaps, wrong odd/even sides, missing values, and MSAG mismatches before you submit.
    Works with GeoPackage, file geodatabase, shapefile, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, SQL Server, and other QGIS data sources. Every edit is undoable. Pure PyQGIS, no extra installs. Runs on QGIS 3.34 or newer, including QGIS 4.
